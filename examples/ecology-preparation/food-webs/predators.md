# What happens when a predator disappears?

Removing a predator can change its prey populations and indirectly affect other organisms in the food web. The outcome depends on the surrounding feeding relationships, so a prediction should be checked against observations rather than assumed.

[NOAA: aquatic food webs](https://www.noaa.gov/education/resource-collections/marine-life/aquatic-food-webs).
